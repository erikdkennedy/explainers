Hydrogen atom,
excited state

Phantom copies of the photon leave in every possible direction

…And at every possible time

…The atom, too, has phantom copies. One for each possible time that it drops energy (from losing a photon)

When the system regains contact with the outside world, we find it in only one state – i.e. there’s only one photon!

Time since the atom was excited. Phantom copies of the photon stream outwards as time runs on, and the atom fades from its excited state into its ground state.
